The Acroyoga Learning Lab teaches acroyoga in NYC.

Acroyoga is partner acrobatics, where students lift each other into the air as part of a trio: base, flyer, and spotter.

The program is unlike a typical group class. You'll walk into a room where every trio is working on something different. You'll follow your own instructor-assigned path, joining different trios as you progress through the curriculum at your own pace.

What is acroyoga?

Acroyoga is about as far as you can get from breathing and stretching by yourself on a mat. It’s essentially climbing around on top of each other.

It’s typically practiced in trios: a base doing the lifting, a flyer in the air, and a spotter keeping things safer.

Benefit - Functional Fitness

Weights that giggle and laugh when you move them.

Lifting people and flying challenges you in complex ways to develop applied strength, coordination, and balance. You build a body that can do things—for adventures now and longevity later.

Benefit - Improve Communication

Have a partner who needs to communicate better? Could it be you?

Gravity is a fast teacher. Don't speak up about what you need? You crash. Don't communicate clearly? You crash.

Acroyoga gives you immediate, physical feedback until speaking up, listening, and adjusting together become habits you carry into your relationship(s).

Benefit - Play As A Superpower

For once, do something with no grown-up purpose. Climb around on people because it's fun.

Play gives your mind a break from constantly achieving—and it's one of the most powerful ways humans learn.

One-Year Acroyoga Program - Novice to Intermediate

Start Here: Online Orientation — 30-60 minutes
Complete the orientation before your first in-person class.

Then progress through six levels:

  • Level 1: Technique Foundations — 1-2 classes
  • Level 2: Fundamental Poses — 5-10 classes
  • Level 3: Side Star — Balance, Entries, and Exits — 3-6 classes
  • Level 4: Inversions — 5-10 classes
  • Level 5: Beginner Washing Machines (Flows) — 14 classes
  • Level 6: Intermediate Washing Machines (Flows) — 13 classes

Bonus: Optional acroyoga day trips and photoshoots

The Learning Lab Format

This isn't an open-level class where students at different levels all do the same thing. You'll walk into a room where each trio works on a different level.

Your instructor tracks your progress and assigns you your own training schedule: what to work on next and which trios to join. The app puts that schedule in your hand, shows where you are in the curriculum, and gives you the lesson notes and videos you need.

Why Donations Matter

Each class costs $105–$162 just to rent the studio, depending on how many people attend. On top of that, the program has ongoing costs for outreach and running the program.

We could cover those costs by charging $45 per class, like many pole and specialty fitness classes do. But then fewer people would show up—which means fewer potential partners for you.

In acroyoga, that matters. As you progress, you need other students around your level. The smaller the class, the more likely it is that nobody else is working on what you're ready to practice next.

That's why the Learning Lab runs on donations. Keeping the class accessible brings more people into the room, gives you more potential partners, and makes it more likely that someone is at your level.

If you can afford to donate, we depend on your contribution to keep that model going.

FAQ

Do I need a partner?
No. Come solo. Even if you bring a partner, you’ll usually learn faster by working with different people—each person moves differently and teaches you something new.

Is it beginner friendly?
Yes. The online orientation walks you through everything you need before class. When you arrive, an experienced student will help you get started.

Do I need a phone and the app?
Yes. The app is part of how the Learning Lab works. With hundreds of students at different points in the curriculum, your instructor can’t remember what you did five classes ago. The app tracks your progress, tells you what to work on next, and gives you the lesson notes and videos.

Can I skip the online orientation and just show up?
No. We used to start every class with an orientation for newcomers. Return students had to sit through the same introduction again, while new students got rushed through everything at once. Now class starts with acroyoga immediately. You show up, your instructor says “Go!”, and you know how to participate. The online orientation makes that possible by teaching you how the program works and the important safety rules before your first class.

The online orientation answers all your questions, with optional modules covering anything else you might want to know.
